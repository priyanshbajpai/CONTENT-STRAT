# Datasets

Dataset and corpus references for FMCG-OS research.

## FoodSky / FoodEarth

- GitHub: https://github.com/LanceZPF/FoodSky
- Zenodo: https://zenodo.org/records/14892842
- Paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.10261
- Use: food-domain reasoning, corpus inspiration, food Q&A benchmark ideas
- Status: candidate source; review access and licensing before commercial use

## Global Gastronomic Culinary Dataset

- GitHub: https://github.com/IS2AI/Global-Gastronomic-Culinary-Dataset
- Use: global cuisine/category references, visual food category research, content inspiration
- Status: candidate source

## Open Food Facts Recipe Estimator Metrics

- GitHub: https://github.com/openfoodfacts/recipe-estimator-metrics
- Use: ingredient percentage estimation evaluation, formulation metric inspiration
- Status: candidate source

## Rule

Use datasets as research aids, not as final commercial claims. Any production health, nutrition, regulation, or safety claim needs separate validation.
