# Visual References

Marcha visual language:

- Contemporary studio commercial food photography
- 1970s hard-flash cookbook documentation
- Early 2020s pop-art commercial minimalism
- Hard direct flash or hard direct sunlight
- Crisp, dark, elongated shadows
- Deep focus, sharp edges, tactile food detail
- High-saturation matte colour blocking
- Minimal compositions with natural negative space
- One solid matte background colour per slide

Background rules:

- Do not use dual-colour backgrounds.
- Do not use split, half-and-half, curved-block, gradient, or multi-panel backgrounds.
- Different slides in one carousel can use different Marcha brand colours.
- Cover images should use the assigned colour for their content pillar.

Preferred scene types:

- Food hero close-ups
- Ingredient still lifes
- Faceless hand action
- Sauce pours, dips, drizzles, spreads
- Tearing, sprinkling, plating
- Clean studio backgrounds

Avoid:

- Dark moody restaurant lighting
- Rustic wood tables
- Soft focus, haze, dreamy blur
- Fake AI typography
- Impossible food shapes
- Plastic-looking sauces
- Busy lifestyle scenes
- Heavy stickers, filled text blocks, or CTA buttons

Food realism metrics:

- Texture: smooth, coarse, grainy, aerated, crisp, charred, blistered, fibrous, creamy, crunchy
- Colour: ingredient-accurate, not neon or fantasy
- Shape: physically plausible food geometry, natural edges, believable folds, real spoon ridges
- Viscosity: spoonable, pourable ribbon, slow drip, spreadable, set, thin, thick, glossy, separated oil
- Surface: spice flecks, oil sheen, seed texture, herb texture, bubbles, toasted edges, crumb

Output rule: generate clean visuals without baked-in text, then create separate text-placement guides.
