## Marcha Research Gate

Before creating Marcha concepts, check current signals across:

- Indian FMCG and sauce/condiment launches
- Quick-commerce shopper behaviour
- Indian Gen Z food and creator formats
- Healthy-ish and high-protein food trends
- Vegetarian and plant-forward convenience formats
- Premium editorial food content references

The research step should produce usable creative direction, not generic trend filler.
