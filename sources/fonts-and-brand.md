# Fonts And Brand

## Brand

- Name: Marcha
- Direction: AI-first food content page that can evolve into a healthy Indian sauces and FMCG product brand
- Positioning: Indian pantry made global; healthy Indian sauces; premium editorial food content; product discovery
- Audience: Indian Gen Z foodies, healthy eating audience, gym/high-protein audience, urban quick-commerce shoppers

## Typography

- Display: Paquito Bold
- Subhead: Paquito Regular or Paquito Medium
- Body and labels: Inter, DM Sans, Helvetica, Canva Sans, or another clean sans-serif

Local font notes:

- Durable type guidance: `work/brand-assets/BRAND_TYPE_SYSTEM.md`
- Brand tokens: `work/brand-assets/brand_tokens.json`
- Font binaries should stay local unless license and distribution are explicitly reviewed.

## Colours

- Deep Teal: `#0F6F78`
- Cream: `#FFF4D8`
- Chilli Red: `#D7382F`
- Turmeric Yellow: `#F2B544`
- Ink Black: `#17120F`

## Format Rules

- Carousel: `1080 x 1350`
- Single feed image: `1080 x 1350`
- Reel or story: `1080 x 1920`

Every Marcha output should include clean visuals, text-placement guides, `COPY.md`, `ASSETS.md`, and contact sheets for review.
