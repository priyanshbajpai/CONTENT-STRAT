# Marcha Source Bank For FMCG-OS

This is the master handoff file for future Codex threads in the `FMCG-OS` project. Read this before creating any Marcha concept, carousel, reel, single image, product idea, recipe, sauce, or FMCG research output.

## Non-Negotiable Instruction

The system in future threads should behave exactly like this Marcha working chat:

- research before concept creation
- food realism before visual beauty
- premium editorial copy, never generic food influencer copy
- hard-flash commercial food photography style
- clean visuals without baked-in text
- separate text-placement guides with typography specs
- fixed aspect ratios and pixel validation
- every output organized into its own folder

## Brand Identity

- Brand name: Marcha
- Brand type: AI-first food content page that can evolve into a healthy Indian sauces and FMCG products brand.
- Core idea: A food product lab in public.
- Positioning: Modern Indian food ideas for people who want flavour, protein, clean-label convenience, and product-worthy food.
- Long-term goal: Build an audience, test food concepts through content, identify products people would buy, then create sauces or pantry FMCG products.

## Audience

- Indian Gen Z foodies
- Healthy eating audience
- Gym and high-protein audience
- Urban quick-commerce shoppers
- People bored of ketchup, mayo, generic chutney, and sad healthy food
- People who like premium editorial food pages, startup/FMCG content, recipe pages, and modern Indian food creators

## Food Rules

- Vegetarian only by default
- Egg allowed
- Fully plant-based variants encouraged
- High protein where natural
- Clean label where possible
- Better-for-you, not diet content
- Safe, edible, and product-believable
- Use only ingredients commonly available in India
- Premium supermarket ingredients are allowed
- Global formats are allowed only when ingredients can be found in India or substituted sensibly

## Visual Style Language

Use the original image style language shared in this chat. Do not replace it with a new aesthetic.

The Marcha image style is:

- contemporary studio commercial food photography
- 1970s hard-flash cookbook documentation
- early 2020s pop-art commercial minimalism
- hard direct flash or hard direct sunlight
- crisp, dark, elongated shadows
- extremely sharp edges
- deep focus, no dreamy blur
- high-saturation matte color blocking
- tactile clarity: sauce gloss, oil sheen, crisp food, real herbs, spice flecks
- minimalist compositions with lots of negative space
- close-up food action
- faceless hand gestures
- ingredient still lifes
- sauce pours, dips, drizzles, spreads, tearing, sprinkling, plating
- clean studio background, never a cluttered kitchen

Avoid:

- dark moody restaurant lighting
- rustic wood tables
- smoky backgrounds
- soft focus
- haze
- fake AI typography
- impossible food shapes
- plastic-looking sauces
- neon fantasy colors
- busy lifestyle scenes
- heavy graphic stickers
- big CTA buttons
- text blocks that feel pasted on top

## Text Style Language

Text should feel merged with the image, not added as a separate poster layer.

Preferred text behavior:

- text sits directly in natural negative space
- editorial menu/page layout feeling
- small premium microcopy
- thin callout lines
- subtle page numbers
- small wordmark placement
- menu-ticket boxes only when very thin and minimal
- body copy should feel calm and intentional

Do not use:

- big filled text blocks
- sticker labels
- heavy rounded cards
- generic social media headline boxes
- CTA buttons
- cluttered overlays

## Fixed Aspect Ratios

These are locked.

- Carousel: `1080 x 1350`, 4:5
- Single feed image: `1080 x 1350`, 4:5
- Reel / short video: `1080 x 1920`, 9:16
- Story: `1080 x 1920`, 9:16

Always validate exported pixel dimensions. If generation returns the wrong size, crop and resize to the locked format before delivery.

## Carousel Output Rule

Every Marcha carousel must be delivered as:

- 7 clean visual slides with no text, no logos, no labels, no watermarks
- 7 matching text-placement guide images
- `COPY.md` with exact copy and typography specs
- `ASSETS.md` listing all files
- contact sheets for review

The user will manually add text using the placement guide.

## Text Placement Guide Requirements

Every guide must specify:

- slide number
- exact copy
- font family
- font size in px
- font color and hex code
- font style: bold, regular, italics, underline
- font effects: none, subtle shadow, opacity, outline, letter spacing, line height
- placement zone
- callout line behavior when relevant

Default specs:

- Display headline: Paquito Bold, 64-92 px, `#17120F` or `#FFF4D8`, no underline, no shadow unless required
- Subhead: Paquito Regular, 28-38 px, line height 1.05-1.15
- Body: Inter / DM Sans, 22-30 px, regular or semibold, line height 1.15-1.25
- Microcopy / labels: Inter / DM Sans, 14-20 px, regular
- Page number: Inter / DM Sans, 16-20 px
- Callout lines: 1 px, thin, low-contrast but readable

## Copywriting Voice

Marcha copy should feel:

- premium
- editorial
- concise
- sensory
- confident
- modern Indian
- product-minded
- useful

Avoid:

- generic food influencer phrases
- long visual copy
- medical claims
- fake urgency
- meme-heavy language
- “AI made this” as the main hook
- overexplaining

Good Marcha phrases:

- Not ketchup.
- Should this exist?
- Indian pantry. Global format.
- A sauce built for momos, rolls, fries, and dosa.
- Healthy food should not taste like punishment.
- Chutney power, bottle format.
- Would you buy this?

## Copy Structure

Use this structure often:

1. Name the tension.
2. Reveal the concept.
3. Make it useful.
4. Ask a product question.

Carousel beat structure:

1. Hook cover
2. The idea
3. Ingredients
4. Process / texture
5. Use cases
6. Flavour logic / product logic
7. Ask / CTA

   - Examples: Kashmiri Garlic Tomato Chilli, Tomato Tadka Sauce, Pizza Masala Sauce, Tamarind Date BBQ, Curry Leaf Hot Sauce.

   - International formats made with Indian ingredients.
   - Examples: Makhani hummus, dosa quesadilla, rajma burrito bowl, chaat tacos, palak pesto pasta.

   - Indian flavours imagined as FMCG products.
   - Examples: pani puri drizzle, butter masala dip, bhel protein crunch, rasam cooler, chaat seasoning sauce.

4. Healthy But Not Sad
   - Better-for-you Indian food that remains craveable.
   - Examples: high-protein chaat, creamless makhani, millet pizza, no-refined-sugar dessert bites, hung curd dips.

   - Educational food design content.

6. One Sauce, Five Foods
   - A sauce tested across multiple use cases.
   - Examples: momos, fries, dosa, rolls, sandwiches, Maggi, pasta.

   - Single-image posts for brand memory.
   - Examples: “Not Ketchup.” “Makhani Hummus.” “Would you buy this?” “Indian pantry. Global format.”

## Research Gate

Before creating any concept, check current internet and trend signals. This is mandatory.

Research should include, when relevant:

- India FMCG trends
- Indian quick-commerce behavior
- Gen Z food behavior
- sauce, condiment, dip, spread, chutney, snack launches
- high-protein / clean-label / better-for-you signals
- food social media trends
- global fusion flavors that can work in India
- restaurant menu signals
- product availability in India

Use research to decide:

- cultural relevance
- product potential
- whether people would save/share/comment
- whether it feels new but still familiar
- whether the food can be made in reality
- whether the visual can stop the scroll in one second

Mention trend logic briefly in the output, but do not make the post feel like a research report.

## Realism Gate For Food Images

Before generating any food visual, define:

- what the food is made of
- what the sauce thickness should be
- exact food color
- gloss level
- spice/herb texture
- likely plating and portion
- how it would physically behave during pouring/dipping

Prompt should include:

- thick / pourable / glossy / chunky / creamy / roasted / charred / crisp / fresh
- actual color notes
- oil sheen
- spice flecks
- seed texture
- herb garnish
- toasted edges
- realistic portion

Reject or regenerate if:

- sauce looks plastic
- food color is impossible
- object anatomy is wrong
- hand gesture is broken
- food does not match the copy
- visual is generic
- slide has wrong aspect ratio
